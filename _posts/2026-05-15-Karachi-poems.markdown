Title: Lonely

```

I may not see you,

I will miss you,
Pink lips, green eyes,

I can't lie,
My heart wants you,

Strum my guitar,
Drink in a bar,

Your face appears,
When drink disappears,
```

Title: Spiritual Man

```
We all start as Muhammad,
In darkness and orphaned,

We evolve to be Jesus,
Forgive thy enemies,

Then find Bhudda,
In a state of nirvana,

Siddartha was the same,
A boy against the world,

Went into the wild,
Find meaning of life,

Found a girl, fell in love,
But that wasn't enough,

Found a job, made money,
But that wasn't enough,

The circle of life,
Turns on and on,

Born to lose,
Live to win,
```
